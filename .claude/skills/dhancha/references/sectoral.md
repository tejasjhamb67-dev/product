# Sectoral lens

Purpose: place the company in its industry context before judging its numbers.
The same ratio means different things in different sectors, and in a
concentrated portfolio the sector call is often bigger than the stock call.

## The four questions (answer all four)

1. **Cycle position** — Is this sector early-cycle, mid-cycle, peak, or in a
   downturn? Evidence: capacity utilisation, pricing/realisation trends, order
   books, credit growth, inventory levels — whichever applies (see playbooks).
   Say explicitly whether current profits look over- or under-earned vs.
   mid-cycle.
2. **Structure** — Consolidated or fragmented? Who holds pricing power
   (suppliers, producers, channel, customer)? Import/export exposure and
   currency sensitivity. Regulatory intensity.
3. **Policy & flows** — Live government policy (PLI, tariffs, GST changes,
   budget capex allocations, export incentives, sector-specific regulation:
   RBI/SEBI/IRDAI/TRAI/CERC/NPPA...). FII/DII sector positioning if findable.
4. **Relative performance** — Relevant NIFTY sectoral index vs NIFTY 50 over
   3m / 1y / 3y, and this stock vs its sector index. A great company in a
   derating sector still bleeds.

## Peer table

Build a table of the 3–5 closest listed peers with: mcap, revenue growth
(3-yr), operating margin, ROCE, D/E, P/E (or the sector's valuation metric),
and the sector KPI that matters most. State where the company ranks and
whether its valuation premium/discount to peers is justified by its metrics.

## Sector playbooks — the KPIs that actually matter

Use the block matching the company's industry. If the sector isn't listed,
derive the equivalent: "what one or two operating metrics drive profit here?"

**Banks**: NIM, credit growth vs system, GNPA/NNPA + trend, PCR, CASA ratio,
CAR, credit cost, RoA/RoE, branch vs digital mix. Valuation: P/B vs RoE.
Watch: RBI rate cycle, deposit competition, unsecured-lending regulation.

**NBFCs / HFCs**: AUM growth, NIM/spread, GNPA stage-3, cost of borrowing +
liability mix, capital adequacy, RoA. Watch: RBI circulars, funding access.

**IT services**: revenue growth cc terms, deal TCV + book-to-bill, EBIT
margin, attrition, utilisation, client concentration/top-10 share, vertical
exposure (BFSI/retail/hi-tech). Watch: US/EU macro, GenAI cannibalisation vs
uplift, INR/USD.

**Pharma**: US generics price erosion, ANDA pipeline/approvals, USFDA
inspection status per plant (OAI/WL = red), domestic MR productivity + chronic
share, API self-sufficiency, R&D % of sales, gRevlimid-type one-off cliffs.
Watch: NPPA price caps, US Bioseure/Biosecure-type legislation.

**Auto & ancillaries**: monthly volume growth vs SIAM industry numbers, ASP
trend, EV mix and platform strategy, capacity utilisation, RM basket vs gross
margin lag, export share. Ancillaries: content-per-vehicle, client/platform
wins, EV-agnostic vs ICE-tied product.

**FMCG**: volume growth (not just value), gross margin vs input basket (palm,
crude derivatives, agri), rural vs urban mix, distribution reach, market share
per Nielsen, new-launch contribution, royalty payouts. Valuation always rich —
judge vs own 10-yr band.

**Cement**: realisation/tonne, cost/tonne (fuel: petcoke/coal), EBITDA/tonne,
capacity utilisation, regional demand-supply (region matters more than
national), expansion pipeline. Watch: infra/housing capex, fuel prices.

**Metals & mining**: global commodity price + spread (e.g. steel spread over
coking coal + iron ore), capacity utilisation, net debt/EBITDA (cycle
survival), integration level, China supply/demand. These are cycle trades:
value on EV/EBITDA mid-cycle, never on peak P/E.

**Capital goods / infra / defence**: order book + book-to-bill, order inflow
growth, execution cycle, working capital days, margin in backlog, customer mix
(govt vs private), L1 pipeline. Defence: indigenisation policy, export
clearances.

**Real estate**: pre-sales (bookings) growth, collections, net debt, launch
pipeline, inventory months, region (micro-market matters), business model
(build-sell vs annuity). Watch: rates, approvals regime.

**Power & utilities**: PLF, PPA mix (merchant vs long-term), receivables from
discoms, capacity addition pipeline, RE transition exposure, regulatory RoE
for regulated entities. Merchant exposure = cyclicality.

**Chemicals / specialty chem**: product spreads, China +1 reality vs
narrative, capex-to-commercialisation timeline, client qualification moats,
crude linkage, export share. Watch: dumping, China capacity restarts.

**Consumer durables / discretionary**: volume vs value growth, channel
inventory, ad spend, seasonality (festive/summer), market share, premium mix,
BEE norm changes.

**Telecom**: ARPU trend, subscriber adds/churn, capex cycle (5G), spectrum
liabilities, net debt/EBITDA. Effectively a 2.5-player market — track tariff
discipline.

**Hotels / aviation / travel**: RevPAR (occupancy × ARR) or PLF + yields,
fleet/room pipeline, net debt, fuel (ATF) exposure, seasonality.

**Insurance**: VNB growth + margin, APE, persistency, solvency ratio, product
mix (protection vs ULIP vs par). Valuation: P/EV.

**AMCs / exchanges / capital-market plays**: AUM/volume growth, yield/take
rate, market-cycle sensitivity, regulatory fee pressure. High-beta to market
sentiment — flag it.

**Agri / fertilisers / sugar**: subsidy regime + receivables from govt,
monsoon dependence, realisation cycles, ethanol/byproduct optionality, policy
(MSP, export bans).

**QSR / retail**: SSSG (same-store sales growth), store expansion pace + unit
economics (payback), gross margin, dark-store/online mix. Judge maturity of
the store-rollout story honestly.

## Output of this lens

A verdict: is the sector a tailwind, neutral, or headwind for the next 1–3
years, and is this company a leader, credible challenger, or also-ran within
it — each with the two or three numbers that prove it.
